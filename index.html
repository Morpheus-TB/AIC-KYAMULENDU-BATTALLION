THE BATTALION MOTTO

Bright and Keen for Christ

Our motto is built on the Shield of Faith — "Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked." Ephesians 6:16

The flaming torch represents the life of a Christian shining like a light day by day — a life whose good works glorify our Father in heaven.
The sharp sword signifies readiness, eagerness, and active commitment to God's word and service.
The open Bible stands as the ultimate foundation of truth and faith.
